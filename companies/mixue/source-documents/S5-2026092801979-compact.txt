 | PURSUANT TO THE H SHARE AWARD SCHEME
This announcement is made by the Company pursuant to Rules 17.06A, 17.06B and 17.06C of
the Listing Rules. The Board announces that on September 28, 2026, the Company granted a total
of 12,023,300 Awards to 1,234 Grantees under the H Share Award Scheme. The grant of Awards
is subject to the terms and conditions of the H Share Award Scheme and the award agreements
entered into between the Company and each of the Grantees.

Reasons for the Grant of Awards
The grant of Awards to the Grantees is to closely align the personal interests of the Grantees with
the overall interests of the Group and the Shareholders, and to incentivize such personnel who are
critical to the future development of the Group to continuously support the Group | ’s operations
and long-term development, so as to strengthen team cohesion, promote the maximization of
the Group | ’s value, and to continuously attract and retain outstanding talent for the long-term
development of the Group.

Listing Rules Implications
The Stock Exchange has previously granted its approval for the listing of, and permission to deal in,
new Shares which may be issued pursuant to the H Share Award Scheme. Pursuant to the H Share
Award Scheme, the total number of Shares which may be issued in respect of all Awards involving
issue of new H Shares that may be granted under the H Share Award Scheme and all options and
awards involving issue of new Shares that may be granted under any other share scheme(s) adopted
by the Company must not in aggregate exceed 10% of the total number of Shares in issue as at the
Adoption Date (excluding Treasury Shares, if any), being 37,961,880 Shares. As at the date of this
announcement and after the grant of such Awards, 25,938,580 Awards involving issue of new H
Shares will be available for future grants under the Scheme Mandate Limit. No service provider
sublimit is set by the Company.
To the best knowledge of the Company, (i) none of the Grantees is a Director, nor a chief
executive, or a substantial shareholder of the Company, or an associate of any of them; (ii) none of
the Grantees is a participant with options and awards granted and to be granted exceeding the 1%
individual limit under Rule 17.03D of the Listing Rules, and (iii) none of the Eligible Participants
under the H Share Award Scheme is a related entity participant or service provider. None of the
grant of Awards will be subject to approval by the Shareholders.
